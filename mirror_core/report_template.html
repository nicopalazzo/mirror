Day by day

How "usual" is set. The line and band come from your first 12 weeks with enough messages (process-behavior method: the average, plus or minus 2.66 times the average week-to-week change). A point outside the band, or 8 weeks in a row on one side of the line, is more than ordinary wobble. It says something changed, not whether that is good. They appear once you have 6 such weeks.
